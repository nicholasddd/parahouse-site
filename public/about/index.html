Who we areS.1

Your silent backend. We don’t compete, we enable.

parahouse is a Singapore-based design production studio specialising in roadshows and exhibitions.

We work behind the scenes, supporting agencies with clear, build-aligned 3D visual communication.

We plug straight into agency workflows, especially under tight timelines, large-scale rollouts or lean internal teams. Our job is to extend your in-house capability without adding friction, overhead or noise.

The parahouse differenceS.3

  • We don’t just visualise

    We embed into your workflow and think alongside your designers and project managers. If something won’t work, we say so early, before it becomes a client problem.

  • Approval isn’t the finish line

    Renders don’t build events. We stay involved through coordination and on-site setup, so what gets built still matches what was approved.

  • No fictional renders

    All our designers are trained in 3D. Visuals are grounded in real scale, real movement and real construction: what you approve is what you can actually experience.
