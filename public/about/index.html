How we work with your team

  1. Brief

    Send us the brief, venue plan, budget and deadline. We agree with your team what we’ll deliver and by when, before any work starts.

  2. Concept

    Spatial ideas and layout options for your team to review and shape, before anything goes in front of your client.

  3. Design and render

    We develop the chosen direction in 2D and 3D and render the key views for your pitch or client presentation, in your templates.

  4. Hand over

    Technical drawings and print-ready artwork your fabricator and printer can work from, and the files your team needs to carry on.

Ways to bring us in

  • A single pitch

    Concept, key visuals and renders to win the room. If you win, we can carry the same design through to production files.

  • Pitch to production

    One team from first idea to final drawings and artwork, so nothing gets lost between what was pitched and what gets built.

  • Overflow support

    Extra design hands when your studio is stretched, for one project or a busy season, without adding to your headcount.

The parahouse difference

  • We work under your name

    Your client sees your agency, your deck and your templates. We don’t contact them unless you ask, and we never pitch to them.

  • Design your fabricator can build

    Our designers work in 2D and 3D, to real dimensions. What your client approves is what your fabricator and printer can quote and produce, so there are no surprises after sign-off.

  • Part of your team, not another supplier

    We work alongside your designers and project managers. If something won’t work, we say so early, before it becomes your client’s problem.

Who we work with

Event and experiential agencies, brand activation teams, and PR and marketing agencies that run roadshows, exhibitions, launches and pop-ups. Some have no designers in-house; others have a strong team that needs more hands for a pitch or a busy season.

Questions agencies ask

Will you ever contact my client?

No. We don’t contact your client unless you ask us to, and we never pitch to them. Your client sees your agency, your deck and your templates.

Do you build or fabricate?

No. We design and draw; your fabricator builds. Our drawings and artwork are made so your fabricator and printer can quote and produce from them without guessing.

Can you work in our templates and branding?

Yes. Renders, decks and drawings go out in your agency’s format, so they drop straight into your presentation.

What do you need to get started?

The brief, the venue or floor plan, a budget range and your deadline. If you’ve already started a design, send what you have and we’ll pick it up from there.
