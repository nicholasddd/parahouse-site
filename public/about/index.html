Your agency’s 3D team, without the headcount

↘

parahouse is a Singapore design studio for agencies that produce roadshows, exhibitions and brand events. You own the client, the idea and the credit. We supply the 3D models, renders and technical drawings that win the pitch and get it built.

Your silent backend. We don’t compete, we enable.

We can help your team with …

  1. 01

    3D modelling

    We turn your brief, venue plan and budget into a model at real dimensions, so your team can test layouts and sightlines before anything goes to your client.

  2. 02

    Rendering

    Pitch-ready visuals that help you win the room and get your client to sign off early, from quick option studies to final key views.

  3. 03

    Technical drawings

    Drawing sets your fabricator can quote, build and install from without guessing, checked against the venue’s rules.

  4. 04

    Pitch and overflow support

    When your team is stretched, we take on a single pitch or a busy season. We work in your templates and under your name, and stay out of your client’s inbox.

The parahouse difference

  • We work under your name

    Your client sees your agency, your deck and your templates. We don’t contact them unless you ask, and we never pitch to them.

  • Renders your fabricator can build

    All our designers are trained in 3D and work to real dimensions. What your client approves is what your fabricator can quote and build, so there are no surprises after sign-off.

  • Part of your team, not another supplier

    We work alongside your designers and project managers. If something won’t work, we say so early, before it becomes your client’s problem.
