Your agency’s creative team, without the headcount

parahouse is a Singapore design studio for agencies that produce roadshows, exhibitions and brand events. You own the client, the idea and the credit. We bring the concepts, 2D and 3D design, renders and production files that win the pitch and get it built.

Your silent backend. We don’t compete, we enable.

We can help your team with …

  1. 01

    Concept and creative

    We turn your brief into a spatial idea: the story, the visitor journey and the moments people remember, shaped around your client’s brand and budget.

  2. 02

    3D design and rendering

    Models at real dimensions and pitch-ready renders that let your client walk the space before it exists, from quick option studies to final key views.

  3. 03

    2D design

    Key visuals, event graphics and signage that carry your client’s brand through the space, and pitch visuals that make your deck land.

  4. 04

    Drawings and artwork

    Technical drawings and print-ready artwork your fabricator and printer can quote, build and produce from without guessing, checked against the venue’s rules.

The parahouse difference

  • We work under your name

    Your client sees your agency, your deck and your templates. We don’t contact them unless you ask, and we never pitch to them.

  • Design your fabricator can build

    Our designers work in 2D and 3D, to real dimensions. What your client approves is what your fabricator and printer can quote and produce, so there are no surprises after sign-off.

  • Part of your team, not another supplier

    We work alongside your designers and project managers. If something won’t work, we say so early, before it becomes your client’s problem.
