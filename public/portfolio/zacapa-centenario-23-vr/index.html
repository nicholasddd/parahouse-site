Aged above the clouds. Played in a headset.

How Zacapa 23 is made, turned into something you play instead of something you’re told.

Players walk through a Guatemalan town under the volcano, harvest sugarcane by mashing the controllers, then step into the distillery. Each of the four casks of the solera opens into its flavours: vanilla, toffee and toasted almonds, raisins, red fruits and nuts. It ends at the bottle in its woven cage, with a pour.

We wrote the concept, set the art direction and designed the experience beat by beat, alongside a cinematic film of the same world. In collaboration with Light Trace Studio, Song Youngbin and Daryl Ho.
