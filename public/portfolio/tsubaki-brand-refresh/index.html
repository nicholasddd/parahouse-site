A relaunch you walk through.

TSUBAKI’s relaunch, told as a journey rather than a display, with a digital passport carried from start to finish.

Visitors entered through a lit tunnel of “I am” portraits, passed a field of folded camellias and the styling mirrors, and finished at checkout, redemption and a bottle recycling point beside an infinity-mirror bottle.

The same layout had to work for the public days and a dedicated KOL media day. We planned for both from the first concept through 3D, renders and technical drawings, and it served both with no redesign. The built roadshow kept the render’s tunnel, bottle and red-on-red palette.
