Audemars Piguet
150 Years Staff Party

Brand
Audemars Piguet
Date
October 2025
Type
Brand event
Scope
Event design, experiential installations

150 years, told to the people who wrote it.

An anniversary party for Audemars Piguet’s own staff: an audience that knows the brand’s history better than anyone.

So we didn’t put the story on a wall. We built it into physical installations drawn from AP’s history: a garden entrance under the monogram, a watchmaker’s library, a suspended installation of floating pages under a ring of light, and dining tables set with magnifiers, notebooks and gold armillary spheres.

Every space carried a piece of the 150 years, so the team walked through their own story instead of reading it.
