LVMH Beauty
Dinner and Dance

Client
LVMH Beauty
Date
December 2024
Type
Brand event
Scope
Concept, 3D modelling, rendering, graphics, technical drawings

The 80s, done up.

LVMH Beauty’s dinner and dance: an 80s theme, for a room of people who work in beauty every day.

So we didn’t stop at neon. We gave the night its own identity, Retro Glam: 80s Beauty Bash, and built it as a run of places to play: glowing smiley lamps at the entrance, an arcade wall with pinball, Pac-Man and Street Fighter, a diner booth piled with plush burgers, fries and disco balls, an “icon” gallery wall, and makeup stations with lit vanity mirrors where guests had their look done.

By the time the LED dance floor lit up under the curved “Welcome to the 80s” screen, the room had arrived in curlers, tracksuits and neon.
