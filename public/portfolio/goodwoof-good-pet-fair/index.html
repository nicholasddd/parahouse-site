Goodwoof
Good Pet Fair

Brand
Goodwoof
Date
November 2024
Type
Roadshow
Scope
Concept, 3D modelling, rendering, graphic design, technical drawings

A high street for dogs.

Goodwoof came to the Good Pet Fair with a whole range to sell: fresh meals, raw cuts, treats and a new line of supplements.

Instead of one counter stacked with everything, we built four shops, one for each part of the range: a Farmers Market for the fresh ingredients, a Butchery with a chiller of plush cuts, a neon Diner for the supplement launch and the treats bar, and a Grocer for fresh food and redemption.

Each shop had its own colours, signage and props, so every corner was worth a photo, and every photo sold a different Goodwoof product. We took it from the concept through 3D, renders, graphics and technical drawings.
