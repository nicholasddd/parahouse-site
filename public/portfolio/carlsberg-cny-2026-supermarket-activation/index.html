Carlsberg CNY 2026
Supermarket Activation

Brand
Carlsberg
Date
January 2026
Type
Roadshow
Scope
Fixture design, interactive game design

Shake the tree. Huat the year.

A Chinese New Year roadshow that fits in a supermarket aisle, with a game you play by shaking a golden money tree.

We designed the fixtures: a red backdrop under a lighted Carlsberg 一起發 header, carpet, a glorifier stand and a sample counter. The set had to be simple and modular, so it goes up and comes down fast at each supermarket on the run. All plug and play.

We designed the game too, a take on the 摇钱树, the money tree. Shoppers shake the tree and gold coins fall on the screen. More coins, better chances of a prize.
