1664
Brut Private Trade Event

Client
1664
Date
June 2024
Type
Brand event
Scope
Concept, 3D modelling, rendering, technical drawings

Walk through the brand, out to the bay.

A private trade night for 1664 Brut on the Kinki rooftop, with Marina Bay as the backdrop.

We designed the path so guests passed through the brand before they reached the view: a red-lit tunnel wrapped in the 1664 Brut cross pattern, then a lit 1664 sign framed by the bay, and a curved bar with a back bar of bottles under the Brut name.

One set of files carried it from the first concept through 3D and renders to the technical drawings the build team worked from.
