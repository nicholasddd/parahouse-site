From flower to formula.

Six zones for TSUBAKI’s 20th anniversary, each turning a piece of the brand’s Japanese heritage, and the camellia behind its formulas, into something guests could walk through.

A stage framed by a giant camellia, a red tunnel walk, a curved LED wall, a sculptural product pour, and hands-on product and styling stations, for a public roadshow and a private event with brand ambassador Bae Suzy.

We took it from 3D model and renders through technical drawings, graphics and on-site coordination: one set of files, from the first render to the night itself.
