20th anniversary with Suzy Bae, July 2026
Type
Roadshow & private event
Scope
3D modelling, rendering, technical drawings, graphic design, on-site coordination

A public roadshow and a private event with Suzy Bae, for TSUBAKI’s 20th anniversary in Singapore.

For TSUBAKI’s 20th anniversary we designed the spaces Suzy Bae and guests moved through: a stage framed by a giant camellia, a red tunnel walk, a curved LED wall, a sculptural product pour, and hands-on product and styling stations.

We took it from 3D model and renders through technical drawings and graphics, so the build team had one set of files to work from.
