No brief. Approved in two rounds.

PropW’s first exhibition at TOKEN2049, at Marina Bay Sands, with no clear brief to start from.

Instead of waiting for one, we proposed a decisive spatial concept early: a bar counter under the P mark, a lounge in front of a purple skyline wall, a plinko wall and a logo tower. The 3D renders became the brief, and the conversation moved forward around them.

The design was approved by the second revision. We carried it through technical drawings, graphics and on-site coordination, so the team went straight into delivery.
