Services

Bring us in by the project or by the month. Fabrication, production and on-site installation stay with your suppliers.

By the project

  • Win the pitch

    Pitch Kit

    For selling an idea before it exists.

    • Spatial concept or your concept, developed
    • 3D model
    • Key-view renders
    • Deck-ready images
    • Two rounds of revisions

    Visuals only: no technical drawings at pitch stage.

  • Design the experience

    Experience Kit

    For turning a won brief into a space people move through.

    • Spatial concept and layout
    • Visitor journey
    • 3D design and key-view renders
    • Material and finish references
    • Two rounds of revisions
  • Get it built

    Build Pack

    Added to either kit once the design is approved.

    • Technical drawings: plans, elevations, details
    • Print-ready artwork
    • Signage and graphics schedule
    • Venue submission drawings

By the month

  • Flexible

    For testing the relationship.

    • One-month minimum, then monthly
    • 14 days’ notice before renewal
    • Capacity subject to availability

  • Partner Recommended

    For ongoing support across your pipeline.

    • Three-month minimum, then monthly
    • 30 days’ notice
    • Reserved capacity
    • Pause for up to 30 days after the first term

Questions

Why not just use a freelancer?

A freelancer gives you a pair of hands. We give you judgement: we shape the idea, design it to real dimensions and see it through to the drawings your fabricator builds from, so one team carries your vision from pitch to build.

You’re a small studio. Can you take on a big job?

Yes. You work directly with the person designing, and for larger projects we bring in specialists from a bench of freelancers we trust, under the same lead.

Who do you work best with?

Agencies for whom the work has to be right: launches, brand events and exhibitions where the pitch, the render and the build all have to agree. We take on a few projects at a time, so each one has our full attention.
