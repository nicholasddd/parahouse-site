Questions

How are you different from hiring a freelancer yourself?

Sometimes one freelancer is enough: a few images, with the space already worked out. We’re for when the idea still needs solving. Concept, 3D, renders and technical drawings under one lead, designed to real dimensions, so what your client approves is what gets built.

How quickly can you start?

We reply within one working day and send first renders within 48 hours. Your team sees the first round before your client does. If your deadline is tighter, say so in the brief and we’ll tell you what’s possible.

We don’t have a creative team. Can you be ours?

Yes. That’s what the monthly retainers are for: an experienced events design team, without hiring or managing anyone. Over time we learn your templates, your clients and how you work.

We already have a concept. Can you work from it?

Yes. Send a sketch, moodboard or slides and we’ll turn it into a 3D model and renders. We build your idea, not ours. If something won’t work once built, we’ll flag it early and suggest a fix.

You’re a small studio. Can you take on a big job?

Yes. You work directly with the designer. When a job needs more hands, we bring in freelancers we trust, and the same lead stays in charge. One contact, one consistent design.
