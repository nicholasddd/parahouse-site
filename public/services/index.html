Questions

How are you different from hiring a freelancer yourself?

Sometimes one freelancer is enough: a few images, with the space already worked out. With us, one lead takes it from concept to production drawings and checks the work, so you don’t have to brief, chase or fix it. What your client approves is what gets built.

How quickly can you start?

We reply within one working day, and first renders usually follow within 48 hours. We’ll confirm the timeline when we reply. Your team sees the first round before your client does. If your deadline is tighter, say so in the brief and we’ll tell you what’s possible.

We don’t have a creative team. Can you be ours?

Yes. That’s what the monthly retainers are for: an experienced events design team, without hiring or managing anyone. Over time we learn your templates, your clients and how you work.

We already have a concept. Can you work from it?

Yes. Send a sketch, moodboard or slides and we’ll turn it into a 3D model and renders. We build your idea, not ours. If something won’t work once built, we’ll flag it early and suggest a fix.

You’re a small studio. Can you take on a big job?

Yes. One lead runs your job from brief to handover and checks every file. On bigger jobs, they’re joined by designers we’ve worked with before. You still have one contact and one consistent design.
